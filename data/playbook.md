**Caveats first:** only 24 clips (about 13 distinct YouTube clips per streamer group, xQc-heavy). Shares are almost all null or 0, so they tell us nothing. YouTube likes are missing on many clips. Treat the rules below as leanings, not proof. I compared each clip to the YouTube median of about 1,400 views.

1. **Pick xQc police and courtroom bits as the default.** 11 of his 14 YouTube clips beat the median. Good tags are `police`, `funny_line` and `rp_drama`. Only 1 of his 14 YouTube clips (the "smacked three guys" clip, 9 views) flopped outright. Examples: "cops call about the car you used in a robbery" got 10.7k, "flag your own car as stolen" got 3.5k, and "18 cops… gun hurts your wrist" got 2.5k.
2. **Pick a one-line absurd-confession or self-own moment.** The best clip by far was xQc's "He freestyled himself into a corner and there's no way out" (15.7k views, 3.7% like rate, about 3x the next best). Other winners are a character digging a hole with his own words ("downplayed war crimes in his interview", "told a customer he got scammed out of 20k") or the system failing him (lag or seizure excuses, the judge blaming daylight savings).
3. **Write the hook so the premise is clear in under 10 words, with one concrete image.** Winners: "He freestyled himself into a corner…", "POV: cops call about the car you used in a robbery". Stop using tangled or run-on hooks. The flops were "When you say you smacked three guys and he heard you screamed" (9 views) and "POV: you trash talk the cop on the radio and he's standing right behind you" (10 views).
4. **"POV:" is fine but not a magic word.** It appears in most hooks and gives about median results (about 1.3–1.5k). The top two clips differ: one is a POV hook and one is a plain statement. Test more plain "He/She [did dumb thing]" hooks next to POV hooks. Keep one 💀 emoji. Don't stack emojis.
5. **Aim for 22–38 seconds.** On YouTube, almost every clip in that range beat the median (11 of 13 at roughly 1.3k or more). Clips over 40s were mixed: 4.5k, 3.5k and 1.7k, but also 232, 12 and 9 views. On TikTok, the opposite showed up weakly. The 47–49s clips were the only ones with solid views and like rates (1.0k at 4.6% likes, 1.0k at 5.7%, 360, 344). So treat the length range as a soft preference.
6. **Cut Cinna to proven formats only.** His 47–48s clips ("downed and crew drives off", "beg a stranger to drive you to the bathroom") did about 1.2–1.3k on YouTube and about 1k on TikTok. His gaslight and roof clip got 12 views, and the "rolling his R's" clip got 232 views. Cinna works when the story is a simple relatable-pain POV. It fails when the clip needs context.
7. **Stop picking vanillamace-style "weird situation introduces herself" clips for now.** The one clip got 12 views on YouTube and 438 on TikTok. One data point isn't enough to ban her, so retest once with a clearer premise.
8. **Webcam doesn't seem to be the deciding factor.** The only webcam-off clips were Cinna's weak "R's" clip (232 views) and Burn's brand-new clip (503 views at 1 hour old, which is promising). Webcam-on clips won and lost in equal measure. Prefer webcam-on when the funny moment is a facial reaction, but don't reject a great line because the cam is off.
9. **Watch Burn's hot-dog clip.** It had 503 views in its first hour, far faster than the others. If it keeps climbing, pick more kidnap and absurd-demand RP from Burn.
10. **Humor that worked is dialogue-driven deadpan, not screaming chaos.** Highest like rates on YouTube: freestyle (3.7%), 18 cops (1.5%), trick-or-treat cops (1.4%), lag excuse (1.1%). `chaos` and `reaction` tags alone didn't beat `funny_line` plus an authority figure (cop, judge, boss, interviewer).
11. **Fix the TikTok pipeline before changing content.** Seven clips with 0–1 views on TikTok got 9 to 10.7k on YouTube, for example the robbery clip (0 vs 10,667). They were posted at the same hour as the YouTube versions and were 237+ hours old. A uniform zero points to a muting, copyright, violence-flag or upload problem, not weak humor. Check those uploads. Don't draw content conclusions from TikTok zeros.
12. **Don't optimize posting hour yet.** Winners came from 09:00, 16:00 and 19:00, and flops from 11:00, 19:00 and 22:00. Hour is mixed up with clip quality and age. Post at varied times and test the same clip type at different hours next week.
